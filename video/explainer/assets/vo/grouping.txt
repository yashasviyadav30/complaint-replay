In real life, forty people describe one bug in forty ways, in English, Hindi and Hinglish. Each complaint is embedded with NVIDIA Nemotron Embed. The three most similar open cases are each checked by Nemotron 3.5 Lightning: same problem, or not? On a yes, the complaint joins that case, and the board shows how many people the bug hurts. On held-out test data, when it merges, it's right about four times out of five.
